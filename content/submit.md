We take submissions from anyone: publishers who want their work assessed, researchers who have been
fighting a dataset all month, journalists who need to know whether a source will hold, and the
people stuck cleaning it at two in the morning.

## What we need from you

A submission is short. Send us:

- **A link to the dataset itself** — the download or API endpoint, not a press release about it.
- **Who publishes it**, and where it comes from.
- **What it is supposed to cover** — scope, geography, time span.
- **What you already know is wrong with it**, if anything. This is the most useful part of any
  submission and the part most people leave out.

You do not need to fill in a scorecard. That is our job.

## What happens next

1. **Triage.** We check it is genuinely open, genuinely a dataset, and not already on file.
2. **Evaluation.** Someone downloads it, uses it, and scores it on the
   [six axes](/method/). This takes as long as it takes.
3. **Publication.** The evaluation goes up with the working shown, whatever the score.

We do not send drafts to publishers for approval before publication. We will correct factual errors
at any time, visibly and with a date.

## What we will not do

- **Take payment** for an evaluation, for placement, or for a re-review.
- **Guarantee a score.** If you submit your own dataset, you are asking for an assessment, not a
  badge. Several publishers have found this out.
- **Evaluate closed data.** If it is behind a paywall, an enterprise contract or an NDA, it is out
  of scope. Free-after-registration is in scope and the registration will cost you marks.
- **Review datasets about identifiable private individuals**, however openly they are published.

## Corrections and re-reviews

If you have fixed something we criticised — added documentation, clarified a licence, resumed
publishing — tell us. Re-evaluations are free, dated, and published with the original score
alongside so readers can see the movement.

If we got a fact wrong, say so plainly and we will fix it and note the correction on the page.

## Where to send it

Submissions, corrections and complaints go to the desk. Open an issue on
[the repository](https://github.com/ofdataorg/ministry/issues), or send it however you found this
site.

> One line is enough to start: *what it is, where it lives, and what annoyed you about it.*
