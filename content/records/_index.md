---
title: "Records"
description: "Individual datasets, downloaded and measured. Measurements, not evaluations — no grade, because nobody has read them."
---

Each of these is one dataset, downloaded on a stated day, with what happened written down: whether it
served data, how empty it came back, which columns were dead, whether it kept its own update promise.

They carry no six-axis grade. A grade means somebody read the thing and formed a judgement; here a
machine measured. The portals these came from *have* been reviewed properly, and every record links
back to that evaluation.

A record is a starting point, not a verdict. When one of these turns out to be worth a real
judgement, it gets [evaluated](/evaluations/) properly — downloaded in depth, read against its own
documentation, scored on the six axes and argued in prose — and the record is retired in favour of
the evaluation. Sixteen have made that move so far.
